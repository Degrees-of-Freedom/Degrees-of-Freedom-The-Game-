Voxel Struct — build, analyse, design

Space is a 3D voxel grid with 2 m cells. Every member piece runs from one voxel corner to the next (straight or diagonal); drawing a longer straight line builds one piece per field, and each piece uses one item from your limited supply. A straight run of the same section acts as one continuous member.

  • Beams (steel tube, glulam timber, IPE, HEB) carry axial force, shear and bending.
  • Cables are tension-only, pretensioned to 25 kN, and go slack under compression.
  • Joints are pinned by default. A Moment connection makes every member end at that node rigid and raises joint capacity.
  • Foundations fix a node to solid ground (not water).
  • Run analysis solves a 3D frame model (4 load combinations: ULS/SLS with wind in X and Y, imperfections, tension-only cable iteration) and checks: mechanisms, global buckling (αcr), member strength, shear, flexural buckling, slenderness, deflection, connections and foundations.
  • Remove deletes members or node attachments and returns them to your supply. Swap replaces a member with another type (beam ↔ beam or beam ↔ cable) in place, keeping its ends; Shift+click swaps every member of that type. Both can be undone.
  • Load path (L) shows how force travels to the ground: thickness = force, red = compression, blue = tension, purple = bending/shear; dots flow towards the supports. Inspect a node to trace its route down.
  • Degrees of freedom (F): after a solve every node shows its six DOFs — arrows for translations X/Y/Z, rings for rotations. Grey = locked by a support, dashed ring = hinge, glow and length = how much it moves; ghosts trace the deformed shape.
  • Pick-up levels: weights float in space — connect every one of them to foundations.
  • Flip levels: a structure is given; rebuild it so the orange target member carries the opposite force (tension ↔ compression).
  • Auto-design resizes beams to the lightest adequate section still in your supply.

Tip: pin-jointed frames need triangles (bracing) in all three directions — or moment connections.
