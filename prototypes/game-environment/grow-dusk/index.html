Controls

1 · MemberClick a joint, a point on a member or a quay dot. Points bloom; click one to place. Keep clicking to carry on. Or drag from a point and let go. Members can run between any two grid points up to 4 steps apart. Where points overlap on screen, the wheel or [ and ] picks the one in front or behind.
2 · ConnectionClick a joint to open its six degrees of freedom. Click it again to step its type. Click a point on a member to add a joint.
3 · LoadPick direction and amount, click a joint. Click a load to edit it; wheel over it steps the amount.
RemoveRight-click, Alt+click, or point and press Delete.
CameraDrag empty space (or right-drag anywhere) to orbit, wheel to zoom, Shift+drag or middle-drag to pan, F or double-click to re-frame.
KeysEsc cancels · [ ] cycle overlapping points · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · H help
