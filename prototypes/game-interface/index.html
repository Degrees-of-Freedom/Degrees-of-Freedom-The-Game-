How to play

You build a structure on a 3D grid with three tools, then see how load travels through it.

Connection

A joint holds six degrees of freedom: it can move along three axes and turn about three. Decide which of them stay free and which are locked.

Member

A member joins two connections. You choose which two joints it links, and that sets how long it is.

Load

A load sits on a joint. You choose where it goes, which way it pushes, and how much force it carries.

What the game measures

Two things: whether every load finds a path down to a foundation, and whether the structure has instabilities, meaning places where it can still move when it should not.

About

Hackathon prototype AEC Tech 2026

Degrees of Freedom is a turn-based structural engineering game played on a 3D grid. Players place joints, members and loads, and then look at how the structure carries load and where it can still move. The aim is to make structural behaviour something you can play with and see.

Implemented

  • Standalone environment prototypes
  • These menus and mode setup screens

Not implemented yet

  • Mode rules and turn order
  • Cards
  • Results and scoring
  • Simulation (Run simulation)

The team’s main app (apps/web) has ported these environments and implements the modes. This page is a design prototype for the home and menu screens.
